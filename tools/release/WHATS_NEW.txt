New in 0.8.0:
One CIA to install: nothing to copy.
Online multiplayer: race up to
  12 players. Host, or join with
  the host's code. No server needed.
Time Trials: your best run is
  saved as a ghost to race or watch.
Camera: the original game's
  chase camera; look behind with
  X or ZL.
License Settings: Graphics, Race
  Timer and a map that turns.
Sound: mono, stereo or surround,
  as System Settings choose.
A while holding R no longer hops.
Old 3DS: fewer races fail to load.
